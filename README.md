# One Street, 140 Years — The Bund, Shanghai

A walkable 3D scene of the Bund in Shanghai that changes from 1839 to today. Drag the timeline and watch buildings appear in the year they were really built, switch between dawn, day, sunset and night, and open the numbered markers to read what happened there.

**Live:** https://philippfboeser-del.github.io/one-street-140-years/
**Companion project:** the 3D museum [My Modern China — 140 Years in One Story](https://philippfboeser-del.github.io/my-modern-china/index-3d.html)

A school exhibition on the economic history of modern China. Built as a single HTML file with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) (loaded from a CDN, no build step).

## Controls
- **Timeline:** drag the slider or tap an era
- **Look:** drag on the view; pinch or scroll to zoom
- **Walk:** WASD / arrow keys or the on-screen joystick
- **Views:** Promenade, The Bund, River, From Pudong, Above
